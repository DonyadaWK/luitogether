## THE VISION

LUI TOGETHER connects athletes, event organizers, coaches and sports-related businesses in a unified digital experience.

From event discovery to registration, race results and personal achievements, the platform is designed to make participating in sports easier and managing events more efficient.

### PRODUCT ECOSYSTEM

| ATHLETES | ORGANIZERS |
|:---|:---|
| Discover events | Publish and manage events |
| Register for competitions | Manage participants |
| Track results and achievements | Monitor registrations |
| Explore training and coaching | Share results and event services |

### THE PLATFORM

**01 — EVENT HUB**  
Running, trail running, cycling, swimming and multisport events.

**02 — ATHLETE IDENTITY**  
Profiles, race history, achievements and performance insights.

**03 — ORGANIZER CONSOLE**  
Registration workflows, event operations and dashboards.

**04 — AI & TRAINING**  
Future personalized training recommendations and performance analytics.

### EXPANSION ROADMAP

- Sports communities and coaching
- Race photography and result integrations
- Travel, accommodation and local experiences
- Sports equipment and service marketplace
- Physiotherapy, sports medicine and recovery services
